# Passphrase

Passphrases made by maki's random number generator, from the Electronic Frontier Foundation's
long word list: 7,776 words, so each is worth log2(7776) ≈ 12.9 bits, and six are about 77.

The centre makes a new one; left and right take a word away or add one (four to eight). It's
never stored. The menu's Type it types it into the computer (the keyboard permission), with
spaces between the words, or hyphens or dots (Separator).

`src/words.txt` is the EFF's list (https://www.eff.org/dice, "EFF's Long Wordlist"), the
words without their dice numbers, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 US licence.
